Stable Audio Generator is an AI music workspace for creators. Enter a genre, mood, BPM, instruments, and use case, then generate a playable music demo.

## Free Test Audio

The generator includes a Free Test Audio provider so you can verify the website flow before calling an external API. It does not consume Stability platform credits and is useful for checking:

- Sign-in state
- Site credit deduction
- AI task records
- Audio playback
- Download behavior

Free Test Audio is only for technical flow validation and does not represent real AI music quality.

## Stable Audio 3.0

When you need real music output, switch the provider to Stable Audio 3.0. It calls the Stability API, so your Stability platform account needs enough API credits in addition to your website site credits.

## Prompt Tips

Good music prompts usually include genre, mood, tempo, instruments, use case, and sonic texture.

Example:

> Warm lo-fi electronic music, 90bpm, soft drums, night city atmosphere, ideal for short-form video.
