## What is Stable Audio Generator?

Stable Audio Generator is an AI music generation website. You can use Free Test Audio first to verify auth, credits, task records, playback, and download behavior, then switch to Stable Audio 3.0 for real music generation.

### Recommended Test Flow

1. Create an account and sign in
2. Grant site credits to the account in the admin area
3. Open the music generator and choose Free Test Audio
4. Enter a prompt and generate, then confirm playback and download
5. Switch to Stable Audio 3.0 when you need real music quality

Site credits control generation access inside your website. Stable Audio 3.0 also consumes Stability platform credits.
