## Introduction

Welcome to Stable Audio 3 Generator. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit the website, create an account, purchase credits, submit music prompts, generate audio, preview results, or download generated files.

By using Stable Audio 3 Generator, you agree that your information will be handled as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the service.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

1. **Account information**: name, email address, authentication provider details, profile settings, and account status.
2. **Generation information**: prompts, selected music model, generation parameters, task status, credit usage, audio previews, generated file URLs, and related task records.
3. **Payment information**: order records, plan details, credit purchases, invoices, payment status, and payment provider reference IDs. We do not store full card numbers.
4. **Usage and device information**: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, access time, referring pages, approximate region, and basic diagnostic logs.
5. **Cookies and local storage**: session cookies, language preferences, theme preferences, anti-abuse signals, and analytics identifiers where enabled.
6. **Support information**: messages, screenshots, files, or other information you choose to send when requesting help.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide account access, authentication, and security;
- run the Stable Audio 3 music generation workflow;
- display task history, previews, downloads, and credit balances;
- process payments, invoices, refunds, chargebacks, and tax-related records;
- diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and improve reliability;
- personalize language, interface, and product settings;
- answer support requests and communicate service updates;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

## AI Providers and Generated Content

When you use an external model or provider, your prompt, generation parameters, and related task data may be sent to that provider so the audio can be generated. Provider processing is governed by the provider's own terms and privacy practices.

Free Test Audio is a local testing mode used to validate website flow and does not represent the quality or behavior of a real external Stable Audio 3 generation request.

Generated outputs may be stored so you can preview, download, or revisit them. You are responsible for the prompts you submit and for how you use generated audio.

## Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, secure the service, measure performance, and improve the product. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling them may prevent some features from working.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only in these situations:

- with AI providers selected for generation tasks;
- with payment processors and billing providers;
- with hosting, database, storage, analytics, email, and security vendors;
- when required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or government request;
- to protect users, investigate fraud, prevent abuse, or enforce our Terms of Service;
- during a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of assets.

## Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and enforce agreements. Generation records and uploaded or generated files may be deleted or expired according to storage limits, plan rules, or operational needs.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, export, or restriction of certain personal information. You may also unsubscribe from optional communications where available.

## Children's Privacy

Stable Audio 3 Generator is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## International Use

The service may be operated from and hosted in different countries. By using the service, you understand that information may be transferred, processed, and stored outside your country or region.
