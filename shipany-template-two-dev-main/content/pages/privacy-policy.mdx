## Introduction

Welcome to Stable Audio Generator. We provide AI music generation, free test audio, site credits, task records, audio playback, and downloads. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you use the service.

## Information Collection and Use

To provide a reliable music generation experience, we may collect:

1. **Account Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Name, email address, and information provided during registration.
   - **Purpose**: Account management, sign-in state, credits, and support.

2. **Generation Records**
   - **What We Collect**: Prompts, model selection, task status, credit usage, and audio task details.
   - **Purpose**: Showing generation history, debugging failed requests, and improving the music generation experience.

3. **Device Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Device type, operating system, browser type, and basic access data.
   - **Purpose**: Compatibility and stability improvements.

4. **Cookies**
   - **What We Collect**: Small data files for sign-in state, preferences, and experience improvements.
   - **Purpose**: Keeping you signed in and personalizing the experience.

5. **Payment and Billing Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Transaction-related information needed for site credit or subscription purchases.
   - **Purpose**: Completing payments, maintaining records, and handling support.

## Third-party AI Services

When you choose external models such as Stable Audio 3.0, prompts and generation parameters may be sent to the selected API provider. Free Test Audio is a local test mode and does not call an external music generation API.

## Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect account data, credits, and generation records, including access controls and standard data protection practices. No internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% secure.

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share data only when required by law, needed to protect service security, required for payments, or necessary for third-party AI services you choose to use.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
